# NeoMe

Your NeoDex companion: it lives on the home page and wanders along the NeoDex taskbar. It gets hungry, bored and tired, sleeps at night, dances when your music plays and reacts to what you do. The more you look after it, the more it levels up.

*Version française : [README.md](README.md)*

## Looking after it

- **Click**: a cuddle. Four quick cuddles in a row and it melts with joy.
- **Double-click**: tickles.
- **Right-click**: its menu.
  - **Feed**: it eats and its belly fills up (it refuses if it is not hungry).
  - **Play ball**: see below.
  - **Cuddle**, **Wake up** or **Sleep**.
  - **Rename…**: give it a name.
  - **Put in the taskbar** or **Hide** (the **Show / hide NeoMe** button in its tab brings it back).

## Its needs

Three gauges, shown in its menu:

- **Belly**: it gets hungry again after about ten hours and says so ("I'm hungry…").
- **Joy**: it drops over about twelve hours; cuddles, tickles and games raise it.
- **Energy**: it drops while awake and comes back while it sleeps, or while you are away.

It falls asleep on its own when left alone for 4 minutes, after one minute at night (11 pm to 6 am), or when exhausted. It wakes up rested during the day; waking it in the middle of the night makes it grumpy. When music plays in NeoDex, it dances.

## The ball game

Grab the ball and throw it (or click it): NeoMe runs after it and heads it back to you. Each ball saved in a row raises the combo; after 3 balls on the floor, the game is over. Your best combo is kept.

## In the taskbar

With **Put in the taskbar**, it walks along the NeoDex taskbar. Click it for a cuddle, grab and drag it to move it (shake it too much and it gets dizzy), double-click to send it back to the home page.

## Levels

Each cuddle and each saved ball is worth 1 XP, tickles 2 XP and a meal 3 XP. Each level needs a little more XP than the last; its tab shows its level, the XP bar to the next one, the number of cuddles and meals, its ball record and its birth date.

The cosmetics wardrobe is empty for now: a new series will come later.

## Data and sync

Its name, level, gauges and records are stored by NeoDex, backed up and synced between your PCs when sync is on; the most advanced version wins.

## Install

In NeoDex: **NeoStore → Discover → NeoMe → Install**.

Building a plugin: see the [NeoDex SDK](https://github.com/NDesumeur/neodex-sdk).
